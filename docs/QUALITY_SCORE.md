# Quality Score

## Grading criteria

- `A`: Complete coverage, stable behavior, clear documentation, low runtime risk.
- `B`: Acceptable overall, but with clear weaknesses.
- `C`: Usable, but needs targeted reinforcement.
- `D`: Fragile, lacking conventions, or with much behavior still undefined.
